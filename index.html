Quick reference from class

Gross pay − deductions = net pay. Federal income tax is progressive: only dollars in each bracket are taxed at that rate. W-4 tells an employer about withholding; W-2 reports wages and taxes; 1040 is a federal return. A deduction reduces taxable income; a credit reduces tax, and refundable credits can exceed tax owed.

D · Am I leaving money on the table? 10 min

The Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC) is a refundable credit for eligible workers. IRS estimates for tax year 2022 show about 23% of eligible Colorado taxpayers did not participate. Check eligibility rather than memorizing limits.

A credit can reduce tax dollar for dollar. A refundable credit can produce a refund even if income tax owed before credits is $0. A deduction instead reduces taxable income.

Federal EITC rules · Colorado EITC rules · IRS Free File · VITA free tax assistance

Federal childless EITC generally requires age 25–64. Colorado has additional eligibility pathways, including some workers ages 19–24 who are not specified students. Other rules apply. Investigate the profiles using official information, not your own private information.
